# Math on screen

Set `[reading] math_display = "unicode"` in settings, or choose "Math on
screen: Unicode" in the settings list, to see each formula below drawn as
Unicode. Speech reads the same words either way.

The area of a circle is $\pi r^2$, and its circumference is $2\pi r$.

The square root of two is $\sqrt{2}$, and the cube root of eight is
$\sqrt[3]{8}$.

A half is $\frac{1}{2}$, and a fraction with longer parts is
$\frac{a+b}{c-d}$.

The quadratic formula:

$$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$

A sum: $\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$.

Indices and primes: $a_i$, $x_{10}$, $f'(x)$, and $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$.

Sets: $x \in \mathbb{R}$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$.

A binomial coefficient: $\binom{n}{k}$.

A superscript with no Unicode form stays written out: $x^{\frac{1}{y}}$.
