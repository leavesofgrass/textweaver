# The quadratic formula

The solutions of $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, when $a$ is not zero, are:

$$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$

The part under the root, $b^2 - 4ac$, is the discriminant.

## More to compare

- A fraction inside a power: $\left( \frac{x+1}{x-1} \right)^2$
- An absolute value: $|x - 3| < 5$
- A sum: $\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$
- A limit: $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1$
- A matrix: $\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}$
