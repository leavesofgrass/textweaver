# Reading Guide

Welcome to the **accessible** reader. It reads *every* word aloud, and it
handles symbols such as <, >, & and "quotes" safely. See the
[project page](https://example.org/textweaver) for more.[^1]

## Getting started

1. Open a document.
2. Press **Space** to read.
   - Faster with `Ctrl+Up`.
   - Slower with `Ctrl+Down`.
3. Press Escape to stop.

![Two coloured squares](pixel.png)

### Shortcuts

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Space | Read or pause |
| Escape | Stop |

> Reading is a skill; listening is one too.
