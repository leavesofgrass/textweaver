# Enrollment in the science courses

Each year more students take the science courses, as the table shows.

Table 2: Students enrolled by course and year

| Course     | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
|------------|------|------|------|
| Biology    | 120  | 134  | 151  |
| Chemistry  | 98   | 102  | 110  |
| Physics    | 64   | 71   | 69   |
| Statistics | 45   | 58   | 77   |

The numbers are headcounts on the first day of term.
