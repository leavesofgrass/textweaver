\section{Introduction}
Crows are \emph{clever} birds with \textbf{strong} memories. Café owners in Portland know them well.

\subsection{Behavior}
\begin{itemize}
  \item They cache food.
  \item They recognize faces.
  \begin{itemize}
    \item Even after years.
  \end{itemize}
  \item They use tools.
\end{itemize}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Watch from a distance.
  \item Write down the time.
\end{enumerate}
