===================
Field Notes on Crows
===================

Introduction
============

Crows are *clever* birds with **strong** memories. Café owners in Portland know them well.

Behavior
--------

- They cache food.
- They recognize faces.

  - Even after years.

- They use tools.

1. Watch from a distance.
2. Write down the time.
