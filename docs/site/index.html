textweaver: an interactive guide

textweaver is a reader and writer that speaks for itself. It reads documents aloud and highlights each word as you hear it. It also lets you write in Markdown, with speech feedback as you type.

It is its own project, with roots in star, the Python reader that came before it.

It comes as three programs. textweaver is the terminal reader: you open a document and read or edit it. textweaver-gui is the same reader in a window. tw is the command-line tool: it converts documents, exports audio, looks up citations, and more.

Who it is for

  • Students with print disabilities, such as blindness, low vision, or dyslexia.
  • Screen-reader users who want a reader that speaks with its own voice, or that works well beside their screen reader.
  • Anyone who learns better by listening while they read.

Everything works from the keyboard. Every change is announced. Nothing is shown by color alone.
