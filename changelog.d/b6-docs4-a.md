### Documentation

- The audio export guide says what happens when `TEXTWEAVER_FFMPEG` names a file that does not exist (it is ignored and the search goes on), gives the full error for an unsupported file name, and mentions the "Chapters in" sentence and the `video` and `chapters` parts of the JSON report.
- The CW guide gives the RSVP key as Alt+Shift+R, says which CW settings do not sync between computers, and quotes the keying-on message as it is spoken.
- The DECtalk guide lists the Linux library folders as the code searches them, says DECtalk ranks above eSpeak NG in automatic selection, and quotes "DECtalk could not start".
- The dictation guide quotes the edit-mode question and the "Ready:" message as they are spoken.
- The optional components guide describes the update-check row of the first-run list and the true search order for FFmpeg, Pandoc and liblouis (the environment variable first).
- The speech guide describes the new engine fallback messages ("Speaking with eSpeak NG: Piper could not start."), the real speed preset message, the extra words in `tw backends` lines, and that the rate, pitch and volume keys are single-key shortcuts.
