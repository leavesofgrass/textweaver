### Reading and speech
- **The Braille view's print line is exact under contracted braille** when liblouis's library is found beside its `lou_translate` program. Each run of cells stands over the print of the liblouis rule that wrote it, so end-of-word contractions such as "tion" in "nation" and "ness" in "kindness" show over their own letters.
- **Without the library, the view falls back silently** to the earlier word-by-word pairing through the program. On Windows the library is loaded only by its full path beside the program, never an older copy in the system folder.
