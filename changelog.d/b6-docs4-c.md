### Documentation
- The command line guide says that `--home` goes after a command's name (`tw components list --home D:/x`), because options before it are the reader's, and adds `tw study` and `tw update` to the commands that take it.
- Known limits add that highlight names do not reach NVDA or JAWS in the app, and that spelling and grammar marks in the app have not been heard by ear (JAWS not confirmed). The what is new page repeats the first in its beta 3 entry and adds faster typing in a very long line to beta 6.
- The reading guide gives the highlight granularity default as "both", the real F1 text of the Find next row, and lists JSON, notebooks, SVG drawings and MathML among the formats the reader opens.
