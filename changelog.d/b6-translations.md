### Reading and speech

- **Every message new in beta 6 is in all six languages.** The 140 messages that were new or reworded in this release (mostly the reasons a live source is down and why a join link does not read, then an EPUB's accessibility information, the caption transcript's minute headings, and the names of spelling marks) are now translated into Arabic, German, Spanish, French and Portuguese. No native speaker has checked these translations yet; Known limits says so, and feedback is welcome through Help, Report a problem.
- The French prompt for a table's size gives its example in French ("3 par 2").
